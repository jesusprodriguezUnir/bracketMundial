UEFA Champions League 2026/27 League Phase — 36 Clubs & Standings

The UEFA Champions League 2026/27 format features 36 top European clubs in a single league table. Each club plays 8 matches (4 at home, 4 away) against 8 different opponents across 8 matchdays from September 2026 to January 2027.

Qualifying bands & cutoffs

  • Positions 1 to 8: Direct qualification to the Round of 16 as seeded teams.
  • Positions 9 to 24: Playoff round (knockout phase play-offs, two legs). Seeds 9–16 face unseeded 17–24.
  • Positions 25 to 36: Eliminated from European competition (no drop to Europa League).

Tie-breaking criteria

Ties in points are resolved strictly by: 1) Goal difference across all matches, 2) Goals scored, 3) Away goals scored, 4) Total wins, 5) Away wins, 6) UEFA club coefficient.
