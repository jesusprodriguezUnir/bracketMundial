## Previa

One of the most stylistically intriguing matchups of the group stage. Brazil's precise possession play and collective combinations will be pitted against the directness, dynamism, and transitional power of the Haitian national team. A spectacular atmosphere is anticipated in the stands, with massive support from both Caribbean and South American fans. A baptism of fire for both teams' aspirations.
