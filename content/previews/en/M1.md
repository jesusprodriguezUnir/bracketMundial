## Previa

The hosts kick off the tournament in front of their home crowd at the legendary Estadio Azteca. "El Tri", automatically qualified, will look to break their historic knockout-stage barrier after falling in the group stage in 2022. They will face Hugo Broos's "Bafana Bafana", who make a triumphant return to the World Cup after topping their African qualifying group, finishing ahead of a powerful Nigeria.
