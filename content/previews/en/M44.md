## Previa

Jordan and Algeria face off in San Francisco in a duel that could seal a historic qualification. The Jordanians will rely on their defensive organization and the blistering speed of their wingers, while the Fennec Foxes will look to impose their physical power in midfield. Patience and clinical efficiency inside both boxes will be the keys to victory.
