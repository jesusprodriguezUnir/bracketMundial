## Previa

Uruguay close out the group stage against Spain in a duel that promises to be of the highest tactical level. The South Americans' trademark 'garra charrúa' and competitive spirit will go head-to-head with the possession and precision of La Roja. If Spain can control the tempo of the game, they will go a long way toward securing the top spot in the group.
