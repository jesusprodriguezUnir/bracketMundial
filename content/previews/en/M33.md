## Previa

Group E travels to Canadian soil for what is, on paper, the most demanding and eye-catching match in the group. The German machine will test itself against the physical strength and directness of the Ivorians. Control of the midfield will be absolutely decisive: if Germany can establish their possession in the opponent's half, they will hold the upper hand; but if the game breaks open into end-to-end transitions, Ivory Coast could be lethal on the counter-attack.
