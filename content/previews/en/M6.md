## Previa

The standout blockbuster of matchday one pits two European aristocrats against each other. Carlo Ancelotti's Real Madrid raise the curtain at the Santiago Bernabéu against Simone Inzaghi's tactically formidable FC Internazionale in an early heavyweight encounter of the highest magnitude.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Estadio Santiago Bernabéu** (Madrid) before an official capacity of **81,044 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Ancelotti will rely on his forwards' individual brilliance and explosive pace to unlock the disciplined, fluid 3-5-2 system perfected by Inzaghi's Nerazzurri.
