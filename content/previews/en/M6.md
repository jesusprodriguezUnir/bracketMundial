## Previa

A thrilling clash on Canadian soil. Turkey arrive with their characteristic passion and a squad packed with talent playing in Europe's top leagues, looking to evoke the glory of their historic third-place finish in 2002. Facing them will be the "Socceroos", a physical, disciplined side that is always incredibly tough to break down. Whichever team manages to dominate transition play in midfield will take a giant step toward the next round.
