## Previa

Haiti's debut at this stage will keep their fans on the edge of their seats. The Caribbean side, led by the most talented generation in their history, will seek their first ever World Cup points, buoyed by the vibrant East Coast atmosphere. However, the draw was far from kind: they face Scotland, a team with formidable attacking potential, imposing physicality, and the experience of having contested multiple major tournaments. Pace on the flanks will set the tempo for this debut.
