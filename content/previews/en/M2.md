## Previa

A crucial clash to get qualification on track. The South Koreans compete in their 11th consecutive World Cup (trailing only powerhouses like Brazil, Germany, Argentina, and Spain in active streaks), bringing their trademark electric tempo. Czechia arrive with high morale following a dramatic European play-off qualification, overcoming Ireland and Denmark in extra time and penalties with a squad led by the attacking talent of Patrik Schick.
