## Previa

England and Croatia face off in Dallas in a mouthwatering clash that evokes the Russia 2018 semi-final. The "Three Lions" will look to impose their high tempo and physical power from the off, while the Vatreni rely on their technical midfield and Modric's veteran presence to control the tempo. A true clash of titans that will set the tone for the group.
