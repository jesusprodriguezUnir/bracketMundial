## Previa

A classic of European football transported to American soil. The Netherlands will have to arm themselves with patience to breach Sweden's traditional double line of four. The movement of the Dutch forwards will be key against the towering Nordic center-backs. If Sweden can keep a clean sheet during the first half, nerves could begin to play a part for the Oranje.
