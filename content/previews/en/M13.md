## Previa

Miami hosts an intergenerational battle. Saudi Arabia seek to replicate their heroic exploits from Qatar 2022 with a disciplined defensive shape and lethal transitions. Uruguay, the eternal "Celeste", arrive with their signature "garra charrúa" and a deadly attack to face an Asian side that never knows when they are beaten. Possession will be key for the South Americans, while organization will be the priority for the Saudis.
