## Previa

Arguably one of the most attractive and technical fixtures of the opening matchday. Qatar return to the World Cup hungry for glory, backed by a blend of youth and veteran experience following years of preparation. They will test themselves against Switzerland, a lethal side on the counter-attack and seasoned major-tournament specialists. The battle between Qatari creativity in the final third and Swiss tactical discipline promises to be an absolute spectacle.
