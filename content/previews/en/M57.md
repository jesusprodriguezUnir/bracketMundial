## Previa

The end of the group stage brings a fascinating contrast of philosophies. The agility and crisp, ground-level combination play of the 'Samurai Blue' goes head-to-head with the physical power, height, and direct approach of the Swedes. For Japan, keeping the ball away from their own penalty area and avoiding conceding corners or wide free-kicks will be a matter of pure survival.
