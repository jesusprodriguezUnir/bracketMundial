## Previa

Argentina face Austria in Dallas in a fascinating clash of footballing philosophies. South American flair and creativity will go head-to-head with Central European tactical discipline and high intensity. The Austrians know that standing up to a giant requires defensive shape and making the absolute most of every set piece. A true litmus test for both.
