## Previa

Uzbekistan and Colombia face off at the historic Estadio Azteca in a high-octane intercontinental clash. The Asians bring tactical discipline and a robust midfield unit, while the Cafeteros arrive with their signature individual flair and the ability to decide matches in key moments. Whoever dictates the tempo will have half the battle won.
