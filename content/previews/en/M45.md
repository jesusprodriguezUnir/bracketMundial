## Previa

England face Ghana in Boston in a high-stakes encounter with knockout qualification hanging in the balance. The Three Lions will look to exploit their aerial prowess and the speed of their wingers, while the Black Stars will rely on their imposing physicality and ability to shut down space. Whichever side wins the second-ball battles will have one foot in the next round.
