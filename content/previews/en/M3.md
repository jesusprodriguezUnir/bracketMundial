## Previa

Signal Iduna Park dresses for a showpiece occasion as Borussia Dortmund welcome Villarreal in a headline Tuesday clash. Dortmund will rely on the raw passion of their home crowd to secure an opening win against a Marcelino-led Yellow Submarine renowned for tactical acumen and lethal counter-attacks.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Signal Iduna Park** (Dortmund) before an official capacity of **81,365 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Nuri Şahin will target territorial dominance and wide overloads, while Marcelino deploys his trademark compact 4-4-2 seeking to punish every German turnover in transition.
