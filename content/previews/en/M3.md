## Previa

The Maple Leaf team begins their World Cup journey on home soil under immense pressure to prove that their "golden generation" is ready to compete at the highest level. They face Bosnia and Herzegovina, an always resilient, physically imposing side with international tournament experience. The key for Canada will be dictating the tempo in midfield and keeping their patience against the robust Balkan defensive block.
