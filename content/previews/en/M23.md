## Previa

Portugal, with Cristiano Ronaldo playing in his final World Cup, kick off their campaign in Houston against DR Congo. The Seleção will look to impose their technical superiority and tournament experience from the start, while the Leopards arrive with a formidable physical setup and the dream of pulling off a massive upset. A debut surrounded by intense media spotlight.
