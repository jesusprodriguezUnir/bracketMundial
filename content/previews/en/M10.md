## Previa

Germany, the formidable four-time World Cup winners, kick off their campaign in Houston against one of the tournament's biggest surprises and debutants: Curaçao. The European powerhouse will look to impose their pedigree and tactical organization from the very first minute with their new generation of talent. For Curaçao, simply stepping onto a World Cup pitch is a historic triumph, and they will seek to leverage the boldness of their European-based players to pull off a monumental defensive upset.
