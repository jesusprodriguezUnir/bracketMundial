## Previa

Antonio Conte's SSC Napoli welcome Mikel Arteta's Arsenal FC in one of the most intellectually demanding fixtures of the European season. Two meticulous, passionate coaches whose sides blend supreme tactical structure with elite athletic intensity.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Stadio Diego Armando Maradona** (Nápoles) before an official capacity of **54,726 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Conte will seek to choke Arsenal's central progression with aggressive double-teaming in midfield, while Arteta looks to stretch Naples vertically and capitalize on deadly set-piece routines.
