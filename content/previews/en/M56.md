## Previa

The majestic stadium set to host the World Cup final will provide the backdrop for this spectacular clash to close out Group E. Ecuador know exactly how to make life difficult for European powerhouses with their tactical discipline and aggressive pressing. Germany, on their part, will want to secure the top spot in the group to build confidence and, in theory, earn a more favorable draw in the knockout rounds.
