## Previa

France, one of the heavy favorites for the title, kick off their campaign in New Jersey against Senegal. The French will look to impose their attacking power and tactical organization from the outset, while the Africans, continental runners-up, arrive with a lethal physical block and rapid transitions. A heavyweight clash that will set the tone for the group.
