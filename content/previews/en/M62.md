## Previa

Senegal and Iraq collide in Toronto in a duel that could well be do-or-die. The African side will look to seize the initiative thanks to their superior international experience, but the Iraqis are specialists in dragging matches into a physical, uncomfortable battle. Concentration during transitions will be absolutely vital for both teams.
