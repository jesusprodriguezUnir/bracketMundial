## Previa

A high-octane and highly physical encounter in Philadelphia. Ivory Coast ("The Elephants") arrive with the power that characterizes their football and extremely quick attackers. On the other side, the Ecuadorian "Tri" boasts one of its finest crops of talent in recent years, standing out with a robust midfield and excellent ball possession. This match could be crucial for both teams' aspirations of securing a spot in the round of 32.
