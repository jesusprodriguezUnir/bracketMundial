## Previa

Few fixtures on the European calendar guarantee as much tactical friction and high-octane drama as this modern rivalry. Arne Slot's Liverpool test their attacking machinery against Diego Simeone's Atlético de Madrid, undisputed masters of continental warfare.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Anfield** (Liverpool) before an official capacity of **61,276 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Slot will unleash heavy-metal pace through the flanks driven by the Anfield faithful, while "Cholo" Simeone prepares a tactical trap rooted in iron-clad defensive discipline and surgical breaks.
