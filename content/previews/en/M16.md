## Previa

The impressive Seattle stadium hosts the debut of one of the heavy favorites in the group. Belgium arrive with a star-studded squad and the expectation to dominate from the outset. For Egypt, this opener represents a monumental challenge; the "Pharaohs" will seek to withstand the European attacking onslaught by relying on defensive organization, physical intensity, and the motivation of testing themselves against the best in the world.
