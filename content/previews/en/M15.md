## Previa

FC Bayern München open their European account at the Allianz Arena against Norway's courageous FK Bodø/Glimt. Vincent Kompany's squad will aim to establish early dominance, yet face a fearless opponent that never abandons its proactive style.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Allianz Arena** (Múnich) before an official capacity of **75,024 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Kompany will orchestrate relentless pressure with wide overloads and surging box arrivals, while Kjetil Knutsen demands brave possession play rather than retreating into a passive shell.
