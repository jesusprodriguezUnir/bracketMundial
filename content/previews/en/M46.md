## Previa

Panama and Croatia clash in Toronto in a high-stakes duel that could yield a historic knockout spot. The Canaleros will rely on their compact defensive shape and the pace of their wingers, while the Vatreni will look to impose their superior technique and tournament experience. Croatian patience will be absolutely key to unlocking the disciplined Central American block.
