## Previa

Ecuador arrives in Kansas City under the heavy expectation to take all three points against the modest Caribbean side. However, pressure plays a crucial role in the World Cup. The "Tricolor" must remain patient to unlock Curaçao's deep defensive block, using the width of their full-backs and flair on the wings. A slip-up here would severely complicate the South Americans' chances of progression.
