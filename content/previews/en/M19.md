## Previa

Argentina, the reigning world champions, kick off their title defense in Kansas City against Algeria. The "Albiceleste" will look to impose their signature possession-based play and the magic of Messi from the opening whistle. The Algerians, with their traditional physical block and rapid transitions, will try to disrupt the favorites. A highly anticipated debut.
