## Previa

Belgium wrap up the group stage in Vancouver against an opponent that will make life far from easy. New Zealand possess the physical capacity to disrupt the Europeans' ball circulation and force turnovers in dangerous areas. If the Red Devils have already qualified, it could present a good opportunity for squad rotation, though the demand to secure first place will keep the bar very high.
