## Previa

Prague's Sinobo Stadium witnesses a high-intensity battle between SK Slavia Praha and Racing Club de Lens. Two squads renowned for boundless engine capacity, unrelenting work-rate, and ferocious individual duels bring the curtain down on matchday one.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Fortuna Arena** (Praga) before an official capacity of **19,370 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Jindřich Trpišovský and Will Still share an uncompromising philosophy of relentless pressing and direct transition. Second-ball dominance and individual duel winning percentages will prove decisive.
