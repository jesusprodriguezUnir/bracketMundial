## Previa

A spectacular opening fixture for Group F. A revamped "Oranje" side, with their traditional attacking philosophy and a dominant midfield, take on a Japanese team that already demonstrated its giant-killing capabilities at Qatar 2022. The speed of the Samurai Blue's transitions will test the three-at-the-back system typically favored by the Dutch. Whichever side wins the tactical battle in the center of the pitch will have a massive advantage in Dallas.
