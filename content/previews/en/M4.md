## Previa

The main host of this World Cup kicks off their campaign at the spectacular SoFi Stadium. The "Stars and Stripes" face the pressure to shine at home against a Paraguayan side returning to the World Cup with plenty of hunger after a 16-year absence (their last appearance was South Africa 2010). Defensive solidity and the traditional "garra guaraní" will test the creativity and talent of the American generation from the very first minute.
