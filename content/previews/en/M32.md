## Previa

Seattle is renowned for having some of the loudest fans in the United States, and the atmosphere at Lumen Field promises to be deafening. The hosts will take on a robust Australia side in a highly demanding physical contest. The home team's speed on the flanks will be key to breaking down the stubborn Oceanian defensive block in a clash that could decide the top spot in Group D.
