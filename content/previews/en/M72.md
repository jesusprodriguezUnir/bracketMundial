## Previa

DR Congo and Uzbekistan meet in Atlanta in a duel that could well be do-or-die. The African side will look to take the initiative thanks to their superior physical power, but the Asians are specialists in dragging matches into an uncomfortable, tactical battle. Concentration during transitions will be absolutely vital for both teams.
