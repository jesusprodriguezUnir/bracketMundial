## Previa

Spain kick off their campaign in Atlanta as heavy group favorites, with the obligation to impose their signature possession game from the very first minute. Cabo Verde, debuting in the finals, arrive with their dream intact and a physical squad that already demonstrated in the African qualifiers that they can trouble any opponent. Spanish patience will be tested by the resilience of the islanders.
