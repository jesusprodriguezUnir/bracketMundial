## Previa

The Philips Stadion prepares for a vibrant spectacle between PSV Eindhoven and Shakhtar Donetsk. Peter Bosz's men are synonymous with attacking bravery and high-tempo flair, while their Ukrainian opponents boast technical class and renowned European resilience.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Philips Stadion** (Eindhoven) before an official capacity of **35,000 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Bosz will not compromise: high lines and attacking waves in the opposition half. Marino Pušić will look to weather the storm with crisp passing under pressure and quick switches of play.
