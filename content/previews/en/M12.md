## Previa

Estadio BBVA hosts a clash where tactical discipline will be the order of the day. Sweden return to the World Cup bringing their trademark physical block, exceptional aerial game, and defensive organization. Opposite them, Tunisia's "Eagles of Carthage" will look to rely on their resilience and ability to close down spaces. Set pieces could prove to be the deciding factor in what promises to be a very tight encounter.
