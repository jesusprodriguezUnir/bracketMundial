## Previa

Hansi Flick's FC Barcelona kick off their league phase campaign at home against Brian Priske's Feyenoord. Built on ferocious pressing and rapid attacking movements, the Catalans aim to send an emphatic opening statement across Europe.

## Crónica

The clash takes place at **Spotify Camp Nou** (Barcelona) before an official capacity of **105,000 spectators**. A top-tier European setting for the 36-club league phase where every goal and every point carries massive weight in the race for the automatic Top 8 spots.

### Tactical duel and dugout chess
Flick will enforce an ultra-high defensive line and immediate counter-pressing, while Feyenoord will try to play through the pressure and exploit available space in behind.
