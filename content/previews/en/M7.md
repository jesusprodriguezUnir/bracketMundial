## Previa

Brazil kick off their World Cup campaign as group favorites, with the obligation to impose their signature possession and control from the very first minute. Meanwhile, Morocco arrive aiming to replicate their historic 2022 run, relying on a highly cohesive squad, a tactically meticulous coach, and a fanbase set to travel in massive numbers. "Seleção" will need plenty of patience to dismantle the expected defensive wall of the Atlas Lions.
